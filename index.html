// philosophy

How I approach building

Clarity Over Complexity

Prefer simple, understandable systems over premature optimization. Readable code and clear architecture matter more than clever hacks.

Systems Thinking

Consider data flows, dependencies, and scalability from the start. How will this work at 10x the current scale? What can break?

Practical Over Perfect

Shipped, working systems beat ideal but unreleased designs. Iteration and learning matter more than perfect first attempts.

Measure What Matters

Define success metrics early. Build systems you can evaluate, not just systems that feel good on paper.
